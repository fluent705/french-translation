29+ Countries Served

French is not one language.
It's several.

The French spoken in Paris is not the French spoken in Montréal. And neither is quite the French spoken in Dakar. Each variety has its own vocabulary, cadence, and legal register — and a translator who's only worked with one of them will show it.

France
Hexagonal French
Standard European French

The formal register of government, law, and European institutions. Standard in EU documentation, international business, and French courts. This is what most translators know — it's the baseline, not the whole picture.

Canada / Québec
Français québécois
Canadian French

Distinct vocabulary, pronunciation, and legal terminology — especially within Québec's civil law tradition. Cross-border legal and real estate documents that get this wrong create real problems.

West Africa · 29+ Countries
Français africain
West African Francophone French

Senegal, DRC, Ivory Coast, Cameroon, and beyond — each with localisms and administrative traditions that a France-trained translator will miss entirely. Nene has lived and worked in this French, not just studied it.

Common Questions

Questions we hear
most often

If yours isn't here, send it directly — response within one business day.

Do you translate French to English and English to French?
Yes — both directions. Meliora provides French-to-English and English-to-French translation and interpretation for legal, immigration, medical, real estate, business, literary, and marketing documents. All document types, both ways.
Do you offer certified French translations?
Yes. Meliora provides certified translations with a signed certification of accuracy and completeness — prepared for submission to USCIS, courts, universities, schools, and government agencies. The certification confirms the translator's competence, the completeness of the translation, and its accuracy.
Are French immigration documents required to be translated?
Yes. Under 8 CFR 103.2(b)(3), USCIS requires a complete English translation of any foreign-language document, accompanied by a translator certification of completeness, accuracy, and translator competence. Meliora's certified translations are prepared to meet this requirement. Submission does not guarantee application approval.
How much does French translation cost?
There is no flat per-page rate — every project is quoted individually. Your price is based on five factors:
  • Document length — word count or page count
  • Document type — legal, immigration, medical, real estate, business, or other
  • Certification — whether a signed certification statement is required
  • Formatting — whether the layout must match the original
  • Deadline — standard or rush turnaround
Request a free itemised quote to get an exact figure before you commit to anything.
How long does French translation take?
Turnaround depends on document length, complexity, and whether certification is required. As a general guide: short certified documents (1–2 pages) are often completed within 1–2 business days; longer or more complex documents take longer. Rush turnaround is available for urgent needs. Your exact timeline is always confirmed at the quote stage — before any work begins.
What French variants do you handle?
Meliora handles France French (Hexagonal), Québec/Canadian French, and West African Francophone varieties — including Senegal, DRC, Ivory Coast, and others across 29+ French-speaking countries. The regional variant is factored into every translation.
Is my document kept confidential?
Yes. All documents are handled with strict confidentiality. Your materials are used solely to complete the translation or interpretation and are never shared with third parties. NDAs are available on request for sensitive corporate, legal, or medical projects.
Do you offer French interpretation as well as written translation?
Yes. Meliora provides live French interpretation for USCIS interviews, depositions, legal proceedings, medical appointments, business meetings, and real estate closings. Onsite, phone, and video options are available depending on the setting.
What documents can you translate and certify from French to English?
Meliora translates and certifies a wide range of French documents for official submission, including: birth, death, and marriage certificates; passports and national ID cards; immigration applications and supporting documents; court orders and legal decisions; academic transcripts and diplomas; medical records and physician letters; affidavits and sworn declarations; business incorporation and financial documents; contracts and legal agreements; and police clearance certificates.
